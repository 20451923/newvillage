# Hearthside

A cozy, self-contained village adventure for a modern browser. Open `index.html` to play; there is no build step or dependency installation.

Gather supplies and grow your village, then recruit adventurers to explore the valley with you. The world map has multiple destinations and encounters; the guild gives companions jobs and levels; the lake and harbor offer fishing, trading, and timed sea voyages. Craft trail supplies, complete quests and achievements, plant constellations for lasting bonuses, and start a new village cycle without losing your discoveries.

**Village systems** include resource gathering, building upgrades, rare items, offline progress, adjustable worker automation, fishing and its collection, harbor improvements and voyages, friendly combat, expeditions, guild assignments and experience, recipe crafting, daily rewards, quests, achievements, astral progression, and a first-time tutorial. Progress saves locally in the browser, including up to eight hours of building and worker production.

Use the navigation to switch between village, world map, guild, fishing, harbor, astral garden, quests, and automanagement. The **?** button reopens the tutorial; the sound button toggles optional sound effects; the automanagement page includes a moonlit color theme. Choose **Start a new story** to reset your local save.
