# Hearthside

A small, cozy village game that runs directly in a modern browser. Open `index.html` to play; no build step or dependencies are required.

Gather wood and berries, build a berry garden or woodcutter's nook for automatic supplies, and upgrade your buildings. Explore the Mossy Trail and make a friend, fish at Willowmere Lake, and complete quests for helpful rewards. Your village is saved in browser storage, including up to eight hours of offline building production.

Use the village navigation to switch between the meadow, trail, lake, and quests. Choose **Start a new story** to reset your local save.
